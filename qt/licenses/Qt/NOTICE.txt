https://code.qt.io/cgit/qt/qt5.git/tree/?h=v6.6.3

The single-EXE launcher extracts the shared libraries to:
%LOCALAPPDATA%/97LaunchQt/runtime/<package-sha256>/
You may replace these DLLs and QML plugins with interface-compatible
modified versions. Keep the .complete file to reuse that cache.
Application source and build instructions are in the qt/ directory of:
https://github.com/nnnnooob/97Launch
